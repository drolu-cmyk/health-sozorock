# SozoRock® Health

SozoRock® Health is a non-clinical health access program of The SozoRock Foundation, Inc. It helps people find a clear next step and prepare for provider-led care.

**Care for Every ZIP Code.**

## Our approach

The program supports resident access and community planning. Providers retain responsibility for their clinical systems, medical judgment, and patient care.

**Providers keep their platforms. We help you get ready.**

## Trust boundary

**No PHI. Consent-based. Non-clinical.**

SozoRock Health is not a clinic or medical provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Human review is required before action.
